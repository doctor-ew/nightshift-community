# Community code of conduct

Nightshift welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Be patient with beginners, critique ideas rather than people, and respect boundaries.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, unwanted contact and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and project event spaces.
Do not post student identities, contact details or private event information.
At an event, report urgent concerns privately to the event organizer or mentor.
For repository conduct concerns, request a private contact channel from the
maintainer without posting identifying or sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban
participants as appropriate. Reports should be handled discreetly; confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed where safety or legal obligations require disclosure.
